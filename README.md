# VoilarM

VoilarM is a free Android alarm clock app built for offline use on your phone. It runs completely on-device without servers, accounts, ads, or tracking, and is released under the MIT license.

## How to Install & Use

1. **Install the APK:** Download `VoilarM.apk` from the link above, open it on your Android phone, and allow **Install unknown apps** if prompted.
2. **First-time setup:** Open **VoilarM**, tap **1. Allow mic & notifications** and grant both permissions, then tap **2. Allow background ringing** and set VoilarM to **Unrestricted** or **Don't optimize** so battery savers do not stop the alarm.
3. **Set time & label:** Pick your alarm time and label, and check **Repeat daily** if you want it every day.
4. **Choose voice or text:**
   - **Record voice:** Record 15 to 60 seconds of your own voice to use as the alarm tone.
   - **Type a sentence:** Enter text for Android's built-in offline text-to-speech engine to read aloud.
5. **Pick effect, preview & save:** Choose a voice effect (**deep**, **very deep**, **high**, or **chipmunk**), tap **Play preview** to check how it sounds, and save the alarm.
6. **When it rings:** Even with the screen locked or the app closed, VoilarM shows a full-screen **Stop/Snooze** screen and notification **Stop/Snooze** buttons. Volume steps up every 10 seconds (`20% -> 30% -> 40% -> 50% -> 100% -> 120% -> 150%`), rings for up to 10 minutes unless stopped, supports a 5-minute snooze, and survives phone restarts.

## Features

- Voice recording (15–60s) and offline text-to-speech alarm tones
- Voice effects (deep, very deep, high, chipmunk) with live preview
- Locked-screen ringing via Android alarms and a foreground service
- Gradual volume ramp (`20% -> 150%`), 10-minute max ring duration, and 5-minute snooze
- Daily repeat and alarm restoration after a device reboot
- Dark UI with mint-green controls over a custom navy-to-violet animated wave background (shifting to faster red and amber waves while ringing), using original vector artwork
